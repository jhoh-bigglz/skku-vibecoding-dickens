# Covent Garden, 1830s — sources & confidence

**Status: schematic reconstruction (prototype for a lecture). Not to scale. Not historically verified in detail.**
The 3D model in `covent_garden_1830s.html` gets only the *basic arrangement* of the piazza from the sources below.
Building heights, widths, the number of bays, roof shapes, colours, the size of Fowler's market building, the
positions of carts/people and the "activity level" at each hour are **assumptions or interpretations**, not facts.

Research done 2026-10-07 (quick web check, not archival work). Confidence: **High** = stated directly in a scholarly
source; **Medium** = stated in one secondary/older source, or inferred from wording; **Low/Assumed** = modelling choice.

## Assumptions (not from sources)

- **Size and footprint of Fowler's building** relative to the piazza (here ≈ 250 × 180 ft, placed east of centre leaving an open forecourt before the church): assumed from the general look of the site today, **not measured**.
- Heights (houses ≈ 3–4 storeys over arcades), number of bays, roof forms, colours, chimney stacks: generic.
- Church proportions; portico shown with 4 columns in a simplified way.
- St Paul's portico drawn as two round columns between two square corner piers, under a deep-eaved pediment roof — a simplified impression, **not checked** against the sources above.
- People and carts are **enlarged (people ×1.5, carts ×1.25)** so they are visible in an overview; they are not to scale with the buildings.
- Houses continuing west along King Street and Henrietta Street, and the grid of blocks around the square, are generic filler to give context.
- Gas lamps, lit windows, cabbage-leaf litter, carts, horses, donkeys, people: **illustrative**, placed by a seeded random generator, numbers chosen to follow the *mood* of the text, not any count.
- Sun position: computed for **midsummer** at London's latitude (51.5° N) using local solar time; Dickens says only "a summer's morning". Clock times of the first three steps are approximate.
- The time steps map to Dickens's own markers (¶1 "an hour before sunrise", ¶2 "daybreak", ¶4 "An hour wears away", ¶8 "half-past six", ¶13 "Eleven o'clock … Noon"). The market scene of ¶7 sits between ¶4 and ¶8 and is shown at the "Half-past six" step.
